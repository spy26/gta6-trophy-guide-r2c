GuideEarned

Phase 1 — Story · 60–80 h

Play Chapters 1–4 and the finale. Grab the missable meditation spot in Chapter 1, and the easy side trophies that sit on the story path (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Ten Pin).

Phase 2 — 100% grind · ~100 h

The long tail: 250 stunts, 42 gold shooting ranges, 50+ challenges, 100 vehicles, all 52 areas, money trophies. The in-game 100% tracker (35 tasks) appears after the credits.

Phase 3 — Strangers & clean-up · 10–20 h

Finish 8 stranger strands, the last collectibles (cards, WAiNK, meditation), and the two unconfirmed trophies once the community pins their triggers.

Hidden trophies stay spoiler-safe in the planner: they are recommended without leaking their names (“Hidden story trophy · Ch. 2”). Turn “Show hidden names” off to hide them everywhere.
